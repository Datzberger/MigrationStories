# Getting started with your class project site

**Welcome!** This page walks you through making your own copy (a "fork") of the class project, turning it into a live website, and finding your web address. You only do this once.

## Step 1: Open the class project repository

1. Make sure you have created your own free GitHub account and are signed in.
2. Go to the GitHub repository for your class project (your instructor will give you the link).
3. Check that you are looking at the repository on GitHub, not the published website. You should see a list of files and folders, and buttons such as **Fork** and **Code**.

## Step 2: Fork the repository

A fork is your own copy of the project, so you can edit freely without changing anyone else's work.

1. Click the **Fork** button in the upper right of the page.
2. On the next screen, click **Choose an owner** and select your GitHub username.
3. Click the green **Create fork** button.

Congratulations, you now have your own copy of the project!

## Step 3: Publish your website so it is live

Your files will not turn into a website until you tell GitHub to publish them.

1. Make sure you are on **your** fork (the address should contain your username, not your instructor's).
2. Click the **Settings** tab near the top of the page (you may need to click the **...** menu to see it on a small screen).
3. In the left sidebar, click **Pages**.
4. Under **Build and deployment**, find **Source** and choose **Deploy from a branch**.
5. Under **Branch**, change **None** to **main**, leave the folder set to **/ (root)**, and click **Save**.
6. Wait 1-2 minutes. To watch progress, click the **Actions** tab. When the yellow dot turns into a green check mark, your site is live.

If the Actions tab says workflows are disabled on your fork, click the green button that enables them, then check again.

## Step 4: Saving your specific URL

1. Go back to **Settings**, then **Pages**. Near the top you will see your website address, which looks like `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/REPOSITORY-NAME/`.
2. Click **Visit site** (or the address itself) to see your live website, including any changes you make to your fork.
3. Make sure you are viewing **your** repository and website, not your instructor's.

**Tip:** Bookmark both your repository page and your website address so you can find them easily later.
